The Adoption Gap

Approval is not adoption

In neurorehabilitation, earning a UKCA or CE mark should be the beginning of a device's clinical life. Too often it's where progress stalls — proven technology stranded on the wrong side of a fragmented, risk-averse system while patients lose the recovery windows that matter most.

Where it stalls
Past the CE/UKCA mark, proven technology hits fragmented procurement and payers who won't commission without UK real-world evidence — evidence adoption alone can generate.
How Verada bridges it
The group brings the technology through regulatory entry, deploys it into its own care delivery, and generates the clinical, operational, and economic evidence systems require.
Into adoption
That evidence unlocks the route to market — direct care first, then private medical insurers, then NHS commissioning at population scale.

The Compounding Advantage

Each layer makes the next one stronger

The group's defensibility isn't in any single layer — it's in the interlock. Delivering care generates exclusive, longitudinal rehabilitation data. That data sharpens the models and clinical protocols. Better outcomes win the trust of payers and open channel access. And that access drives more delivery.

Every turn of the loop deepens all of it at once — data that can't be acquired retrospectively, evidence in the formats commissioners already trust, and a route to market that gets harder to replicate with each patient treated.

The moat widens with volume rather than eroding under competition — because the advantage is built from the care itself.

A Repeatable Pathway

Built once, reused for every technology

The hard work is done once and amortised across the whole portfolio. Each new neurorehabilitation technology follows the same repeatable route — reusing the clinical infrastructure, evidence engine, and market-access playbook already in place.

1

Prepare

Study design, evidence-pathway build, and grant navigation — laying the groundwork before a single patient is seen.

2

Validate

Feasibility and acceptability studies that qualify the intervention, de-risk workflow fit, and prepare higher-order trials where warranted.

3

Deploy

Clinic-led patient deployment within Verada's own care delivery — creating early revenue, operational proof, and live outcomes data at once.

4

Scale

Real-world evidence unlocks the route to market — private-provider uptake, payer engagement, and NHS commissioning at population scale.
