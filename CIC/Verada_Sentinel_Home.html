How it works

Three steps, and we do the hard part.

1

Tell us what matters

Answer a few questions about where you live and what you'd like peace of mind about. We suggest the plan that fits, with the price.

2

We set it up

A watch or pendant comes by post. Home sensors, such as the bathroom and under-mattress sensors, are installed for you by a technician in a single visit.

3

Our clinical hub keeps watch

Readings come to our 24/7 clinical hub. A nurse checks every alert, then calls you, or the people on your contact list in the order you choose.

Questions

Things people often ask.

Is Verada Sentinel an emergency service?

No. In an emergency, call 999 straight away. Verada does not send anyone to the home. Our nurses review alerts and contact you, or the people you choose, and help you decide what to do next.

Who gets a call if something looks wrong?

You choose. When you sign up, you set your contact list: who we call, and in what order. You also choose who, if anyone, sees your alerts and monthly picture.

Does someone need to come to my home?

For Assure Safe, no: the watch or pendant comes by post. Assure Home, Complete and Sleep include home sensors, so a technician installs them in one visit.

Can I change or stop my plan?

Yes. There's no long contract, and you can move up or down a plan any month.

Does it diagnose anything?

No. The equipment measures and alerts. It doesn't diagnose any condition. If our nurses think you need a doctor, we'll tell you and help you arrange it.

Is Verada regulated?

Verada is preparing for registration with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). Regulated services will start only once registration is in place.
